Service Details

Laundry Services in Detail

A closer look at each service, so you know what to ask for when you call or WhatsApp us.

Steam Press Service in Surat

Steam press keeps your everyday clothing looking sharp — shirts, t-shirts, trousers, dresses and sarees pressed with a clean, professional finish. It's a good fit for daily office wear, uniforms and garments you want ready to wear without ironing at home.

Dry Cleaning Service in Surat

Some garments need more careful handling — delicate fabrics, special-occasion wear and premium clothing that can't go through a regular wash. Our dry cleaning service is suited to these pieces, helping them stay in good condition wash after wash.

Saree Roll Press Service

Saree roll press gives your sarees a neat, wrinkle-free finish, rolled and pressed the traditional way. It works well for both everyday sarees and the ones you're saving for a wedding or special occasion.

Wash & Fold Service

For regular clothing that just needs to be washed, dried and neatly folded, wash & fold is a convenient option for busy professionals, families and students who'd rather spend their time elsewhere.

Wash & Iron Service

When you want your clothes washed and pressed in one go, wash & iron covers both steps — clean, dry and properly ironed, ready to wear or store.

Blanket Cleaning

Blankets and heavier fabric items need a different kind of care because of their size and weight. We clean blankets and similar heavy items so they come back fresh and properly dried.

Curtain Cleaning

Curtains collect dust and odours over time but are inconvenient to wash at home. We take care of curtain cleaning so yours stay fresh without you having to take them down yourself.

Commercial & Bulk Laundry

Beyond individual customers, we also support businesses — hotels, salons, small offices and similar setups — with regular commercial and bulk laundry needs. Looking for bulk steam press for manufacturing or e-commerce stock? See our dedicated bulk steam press service above.
